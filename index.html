Frequently Asked Questions

Does Right Drop need an internet connection?

No. Right Drop runs fully offline. There are no network requests, no account, no cloud sync, and no Game Center. Everything — gameplay, your best score, and settings — stays on your device.

How do I play Right Drop?

Blocks rotate automatically clockwise, so there is no manual rotate button. You have just two controls: tap the right arrow to move the piece one step to the right, or hold the down arrow to drop it faster. There is no left move. Fill a complete horizontal line to clear it and score.

How does leveling work?

Every 10 cleared lines raises your level. As you climb levels, the fall speed and the auto-spin speed both increase, so the game gets faster.

Where is my score saved, and can I reset it?

Your best score (BEST) is stored only on your device using iOS local storage, and is shown on the HUD. To start completely fresh, delete and reinstall the app — that clears the local score and theme preference.

Does Right Drop request any permissions like microphone or camera?

No. The app requests no device permissions at all and shows no system permission prompts. Sound effects are synthesized on your device with Apple's AVAudioEngine; we never use the microphone, and we do not record, store, or upload any audio.

Is an account or sign-in required?

No. There is no account, no sign-up, and no login. Every feature is available immediately after install.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads and no in-app purchases. Right Drop is a one-time purchase; everything is included and no further payment is ever required.

How do I pause, mute, or change the theme?

Use the top HUD buttons: tap the pause/play icon to pause or resume, tap the speaker icon to mute or unmute, and tap the sun/moon icon to switch between dark and light themes. Your theme choice is remembered on your device.

What are the system requirements?

Right Drop requires iOS 17.0 or later and runs on iPhone.
