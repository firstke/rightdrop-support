# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Applies to Right Drop for iPhone

## 1. Overview

Right Drop is a falling-block puzzle game for iPhone. We take your privacy seriously: **the app works entirely offline and never sends your gameplay, score, or settings to any server.** All processing happens on your device.

## 2. Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information and do not upload any user data.**

- Right Drop makes no network requests and uses no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
- The app stores only two non-personal preferences locally on your device using iOS `UserDefaults`: your best score and your theme choice (dark / light).
- Sound effects are synthesized on your device with Apple's `AVAudioEngine`. We never use the microphone, and we do not record, store, or upload any audio.
- No account is required, and no identifier is associated with you.

## 3. How Data Is Used and Shared

- Your best score and theme preference are used only to present the HUD and remember your appearance choice on your device.
- We never share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
- Right Drop is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases; there is no payment processing inside the app, and we store no payment credentials.

## 4. Data Storage and Deletion

- All data lives in iOS local storage (`UserDefaults`) on your device.
- You can clear the best score and theme preference at any time by deleting and reinstalling the app. The action takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.
- Uninstalling the app removes all related data from your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed specifically to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from any user of any age. Parental guidance is recommended.

## 6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data: it always stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time by reinstalling the app. We retain no copy of your data.
